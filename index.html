01Planning & designFrom your family’s brief to working drawings+

A plan shaped around the way you want to live.

Architecture & drawings +

2D planning, 3D elevation, GFC drawings, Vastu and structural design are listed in the proposal. PDF drawing copies are shared with the plot owner.

Included within proposal scope

How the design process works +
  1. Architect takes a design brief from the family.
  2. One or two floor-plan options are presented after 7–10 days.
  3. Feedback is incorporated before the elevation design.

The proposal mentions unlimited design changes in its overview, while the process describes typical iterations. Agree the change process in the contract.

Soil testing, survey & approvals +

Site survey and soil testing are included. Assistance with approvals is described; government approval fees, permissions, borewell/tubewell and electrical connections are excluded in the terms.

Fees & external connections cost extra

03InteriorsKitchen, wardrobes, TV unit & ceilings+
Modular kitchen +

A 10-ft modular kitchen, subject to GFC drawings, with BWP plywood, laminate finish and soft-close hardware. Chimney and hob are listed.

One kitchen is included per 3,000 sq. ft. built-up area. Additional kitchens are charged pro-rata. Island kitchen and breakfast counter are additional.

Wardrobes, TV unit & false ceiling +

Wardrobes up to 6-ft wide per bedroom, extending to ceiling or false ceiling; TV unit, up to five decorative artificial plants and whole-house false ceiling as specified.

Dimensions, finishes and hardware follow the agreed design and detailed proposal.

What is a custom upgrade? +

Additional wall cladding, furniture, curtains, appliances and expanded interior work are separately priced. The overview and detailed specification overlap on items such as vanity; confirm these in the final schedule.

Understand your priceGST, area, allowances and extras
What does ₹2,765 cover? +

The supplied proposal’s rate is ₹2,765 per sq. ft., plus GST, calculated on total built-up slab area across floors. Ask the team to confirm the current rate and your project-specific quotation.

What costs extra? +

Selections above allowances, additional scope, extra kitchens/toilets, demolition, ground conditions, government fees and excluded amenities may add cost. Finalise a written inclusion/exclusion schedule before agreeing the total.

Timeline & changes +

The proposal states 12 months plus a three-month grace period. Your contract should confirm start conditions, site dependencies, changes and the final delivery schedule.
