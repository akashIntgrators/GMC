Privacy & data use

This page explains how the enquiry experience works. For questions, contact contact@goldmansmarshall.in.

Enquiry details

Name, phone number, plot locality and plot-ownership answer are used to prepare a WhatsApp enquiry to Goldman’s Marshall’s on +91 7676 246810. The website does not store or submit this form to a database. You open WhatsApp and decide whether to send the message. Once sent, the business can use it to respond to your enquiry; WhatsApp’s own privacy terms also apply.

Campaign reference

Advertising campaign parameters in the page URL may be included in your prepared message so the team can identify its source. These remain in the current page’s memory and are not stored by the form. You may edit the message before sending.

Optional analytics

If configured and you allow analytics, Google and/or Meta tags may record visits and interactions such as opening an enquiry link. Your form name, phone and locality are not sent as analytics event fields. A link click does not confirm a sent message or completed lead. Analytics preference is stored in your browser. Use Analytics preferences on the main page to change it. Disabling analytics stops future loading on reload; it does not remove data already received by providers.

Hosting and media

GitHub Pages serves this website and may process normal technical request information. The static map is stored with the site. Externally hosted project media may make requests to its hosting provider when loaded.

Contact and requests

Contact the business to request access, correction or deletion of enquiry messages it holds. Business message retention and operational practices should be confirmed directly with the team.
